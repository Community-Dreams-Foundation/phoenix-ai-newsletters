About this newsletter

This educational resource introduces cholesterol and the major components of a lipid profile, including LDL cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, and triglycerides. It also explains how these measurements relate to cardiovascular health.

Important note

Cholesterol and lipid results should be interpreted in the context of an individual's overall health, medical history, medications, and cardiovascular risk factors. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for interpretation of personal laboratory results.
