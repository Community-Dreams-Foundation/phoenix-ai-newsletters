About this newsletter

This educational resource introduces testosterone and explains its relationship with body composition, muscle protein synthesis, bone mineral density, metabolic health, and sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG).

Important note

Testosterone results should not be interpreted in isolation. Levels can vary with age, sex, time of day, health status, medications, SHBG concentrations, and laboratory method. A single result does not by itself diagnose testosterone deficiency or determine whether treatment is appropriate. Always discuss laboratory results and symptoms with a qualified healthcare professional.
